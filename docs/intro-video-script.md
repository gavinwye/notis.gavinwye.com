# Intro video script

Length: about 80 seconds. Screen recording of notis.gavinwye.com with a voiceover. No face to camera needed.
Words are spoken; lines in [brackets] are what's on screen.

---

**1. The problem (0:00 – 0:12)**

[A page of text. An email appears over it: "The second paragraph feels off."]

"Reviewing a page by email gets you notes like this. Which paragraph? What did they mean? You spend longer working out the note than fixing the page."

**2. Select and comment (0:12 – 0:30)**

[Select a sentence. A yellow Comment button appears. Click it. The box opens with the name field first. Type a name, type a comment. Post.]

"So here's a different way. Select the words you mean. Say what's wrong. Your name, your comment, done. The comment stays attached to those exact words."

**3. The panel (0:30 – 0:45)**

[The panel slides in. Two or three comments listed. Click one: the page scrolls to the highlighted words and flashes. Click Reply, type, post. Click Resolve.]

"Every comment is in one list, in the order it appears on the page. Click one and the page takes you to the words. Reply, and resolve it when it's fixed."

**4. Pins (0:45 – 0:57)**

[Click "Pin on image". Click a button in the wireframe. A round yellow pin appears with the number 1.]

"Can't select it? Pin it. Click the spot on any image, and the comment stays at that point."

**5. Close the loop (0:57 – 1:08)**

[Terminal: a curl of /api/comments returns JSON. Then a split screen: Claude editing the page, comments flipping to resolved.]

"Comments are plain data, so you, a script, or an AI assistant can work through them and mark them done."

**6. Get it (1:08 – 1:20)**

[Code block: one script tag. Then the "Add it with an agent" prompt on the site. Then the name Notis, the URL notis.gavinwye.com and the GitHub repo.]

"One line to add it to a page, or ask your AI assistant to do it. It's called Notis, it's open source, and it's at notis.gavinwye.com."

---

## Notes for recording

- Use the live site, in a private window so there's no saved name and the comment box asks for one.
- Prepare two or three existing comments before recording so the panel isn't empty at 0:30.
- Keep the pointer slow. Zoom in on the comment box and the pin.
- Don't show a real person's name in the name field. Use "Sam".
- Captions on. Most people watch these muted.
